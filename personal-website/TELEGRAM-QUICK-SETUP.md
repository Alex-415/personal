# 🚀 Telegram Setup - Quick Reference\n\n## 📋 What You Need\n\n1. **Your Bot Token** (from @BotFather)\n   - Format: `123456:ABC-DEF1234ghIkl-zyx57W2v1u123ew11`\n\n2. **Your Worker URL** (from Cloudflare)\n   - Format: `https://openclaw-experience.your-account.workers.dev`\n\n---\n\n## ⚡ Quick Setup (3 steps)\n\n### Step 1: Get Your Worker URL\n\n```bash\nwrangler deployments list\n```\n\nCopy the URL from the output.\n\n### Step 2: Set Webhook\n\n```bash\n# Replace YOUR_BOT_TOKEN and YOUR_WORKER_URL\n\ncurl -X POST https://api.telegram.org/botYOUR_BOT_TOKEN/setWebhook \\\n  -H \"Content-Type: application/json\" \\\n  -d '{\"url\": \"YOUR_WORKER_URL/webhook\"}'\n```\n\n**Example:**\n```bash\ncurl -X POST https://api.telegram.org/bot123456:ABC-DEF1234/setWebhook \\\n  -H \"Content-Type: application/json\" \\\n  -d '{\"url\": \"https://openclaw-experience.myaccount.workers.dev/webhook\"}'\n```\n\n**Expected response:**\n```json\n{\"ok\":true,\"result\":true,\"description\":\"Webhook was set\"}\n```\n\n### Step 3: Verify Webhook\n\n```bash\n# Replace YOUR_BOT_TOKEN\n\ncurl https://api.telegram.org/botYOUR_BOT_TOKEN/getWebhookInfo\n```\n\n**Expected response:**\n```json\n{\n  \"ok\": true,\n  \"result\": {\n    \"url\": \"https://openclaw-experience.myaccount.workers.dev/webhook\",\n    \"has_custom_certificate\": false,\n    \"pending_update_count\": 0\n  }\n}\n```\n\n✅ If you see your URL, you're done!\n\n---\n\n## 🤖 Test on Telegram\n\n1.
